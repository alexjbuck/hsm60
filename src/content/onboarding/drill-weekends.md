## Aircrew drill weeks

Aircrew drill in **drill weeks**, not single weekends. A drill week runs **Tuesday through Sunday**.

- Drill weeks are held **about every other month**, so roughly **6 per year**.
- You're expected to participate in **4 of the 6** each year.
- Exceptions are made for time spent on deployment.

See [How to SELRES](/onboarding/how-to-selres) for drill rules, rescheduling and authorized absences.
