Those are the four numbers you need to hit each year. Missing the first two is a satisfactory participation problem and can lead to administrative action (MILPERSMAN 1001-150). The flight hours come with a NATOPS check and an instrument check, and at least half the hours must be in the aircraft (OPNAVINST 3710, p. 11-4).

Check the governing instruction before acting on anything here, and ask admin if something doesn't match.

## Two calendars

Drills, AT and flight hours run on the **fiscal year** (October to September). Retirement points run on **your anniversary year**, which starts in the month you commissioned. The two don't line up, and that catches people.

![Fiscal years and anniversary years don't line up. AT done in October 2025 and September 2027 satisfies both fiscal years, but the May 2026 to May 2027 anniversary year gets no AT.](/images/selres-two-calendars.svg)

Here you met your AT requirement in both fiscal years, but the anniversary year in the middle got no AT points. Drills usually carry that year anyway (15 + 48 = 63). If you also took authorized absences, check your points on BOL before your anniversary month.

## Drills vs. orders

|  | Drills (IDT) | Orders (AT and others) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A 4-hour block, two per day | Full days of active duty |
| You owe | 48 per fiscal year | 12 days per fiscal year |
| Pay | One day's base pay **per drill**, so a drill day pays two days. Plus flight pay. | One day's pay per day, plus flight pay, BAH and BAS |
| Points | 1 per drill | 1 per day |
| How to schedule | EDM, in NSIPS | Admin builds a requirement, then you build orders in NROWS |
| Travel | Usually on you. IDT-R may cover up to $750 if funding lasts. No per diem. | The Navy pays |
| Can't make it? | Reschedule, or get an authorized absence in EDM. Max 24 absences in any 12 months. | You'll need a waiver |

Your IDT orders *are* your orders to HSM-60. Admin prefers you finish AT early in the year. Don't forget your annual PRT and PHA.

**Also worth knowing:**

- **Flight pay** shows on your LES, prorated per 30 days.
- **TRICARE Reserve Select** is available. Orders of 30 days or more make you eligible for TRICARE.
- **VA disability** can be paid while you're a SELRES, but not for days you drill. Ask admin for the squadron's VA claims contact.
- **Drilling away from home base** is IDTT (inactive duty with travel).

## Good years

![A typical year: 15 free points, plus 48 for drills, plus 12 for AT, totals 75 points. 50 makes a good year.](/images/selres-points.svg)

You get 15 points just for being in the SELRES. Drills and AT add the rest, and a normal year lands around 75. A bad year carries no penalty, except that it doesn't count toward your 20. More points also means more retirement pay.

**Where to check:** BOL → ASOSH/ARPR shows your summary. The **NEOPS** report in NSIPS is the official record, and your retirement will be based on it. If you're coming off active duty, update your ASOSH/ARPR.

## Want more work?

![48 required drills plus up to 84 additional drills makes a maximum of 132 drills a year.](/images/selres-extra-drills.svg)

That's up to 66 drill days a year. Request extra drills in EDM.

| Type | What | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| AFTP | Flying. You must be on the flight schedule. Doesn't count toward your 48. | 22 a year, 2 a day |
| ATP | Additional training | 36 a year, 2 a day |
| RMP | Admin work. Can be telework. | 36 a year, 1 a day |

You can also pick up extra orders: **EAT** (extra AT, up to 29 days a year), **ADT** (training), **ADSW** (special work) and **ADOS** (operational support). Mobilization and recall orders don't go through NROWS.

## Where do I do that?

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Find my billet number and PRD | RFMT |
| Reschedule a drill, muster, or request extra drills | EDM, in NSIPS |
| Build orders | NROWS, after admin builds the requirement |
| Get travel paid | DTS. Get an authorization **before** you travel, and file a voucher within **5 working days after**. NROWS creates the authorization for orders. |
| Check my points | BOL → ASOSH/ARPR |
| Send a "don't pick me" letter or other board correspondence | BOL |
