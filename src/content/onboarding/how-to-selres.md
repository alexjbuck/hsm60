## The short version

- **Drills.** A drill is a 4-hour block. You owe **48 a year**. Aircrew do them in Tuesday–Sunday **drill weeks** about every other month, and you're expected at **4 of the 6**. Can't make one? Reschedule it or get an excused absence in EDM. Don't just skip it. [Details](#drills-idt)
- **Two weeks of active duty (AT).** You owe **12 days** of active duty each year. Tell admin, and they'll set it up so you can build orders in NROWS. [Details](#active-duty-orders-at)
- **Stay current to fly.** **100 flight hours** a year, including 12 night and 12 instrument, plus your NATOPS and instrument checks. [Details](#4-meet-3710-requirements)
- **Retirement counts "good years".** A good year is **50 points**. You get 15 free, 1 per drill and 1 per day of orders, so a normal year of drills plus AT (15 + 48 + 12 = 75) clears it. Your year starts in the month you commissioned, not in October, so check your points. [Details](#5-qualifying-years-toward-retirement)
- **Want more work?** There are extra drills (flying, training, admin) and extra orders, up to set limits. [Details](#6-additional-participation)
- **Pay.** Each drill pays one day's base pay (1/30 of a month) plus flight pay. Orders pay daily, and also cover housing and food allowances. [Details](#pay-and-benefits)
- **Travel.** It all runs through DTS. Get an authorization **before** you go, and file your voucher within **5 working days** after. [Details](#pay-and-benefits)
- **Promotions and paperwork.** Watch board dates and FITREP due dates, and keep your record current on BOL. [Details](#career-and-advancement)
- **First things to do.** Set up Flank Speed/NVD, get a SIPR token, and do your GMTs and ethics training. [Details](#to-do-list-for-new-jaguars)

## The details

Transcribed from the "How to SELRES" whiteboard in the ready room. Items marked **(verify)** were hard to read; confirm them with admin. Always check the governing instruction before acting on anything here.

## 1. Affiliate

Selection boards. If you are reading this, you have already done this step.

## 2. Orders to HSM-60

Go to **RFMT**. There you can find your **billet #** and **PRD**. Your IDT orders *are* your orders to HSM-60. Ask admin for the squadron UIC.

## 3. Satisfactory participation (fiscal year)

Measured by **fiscal year** (Oct–Sep). Ref: **MILPERSMAN 1001-150**. Failing this requirement can result in administrative action.

### Drills (IDT)

- Two 4-hour drill periods per day.
- Aircrew drill in Tuesday–Sunday **drill weeks**, about every other month. See [Drill weeks](/onboarding/drill-weekends).
- You are scheduled for **48 drill periods each FY**: 12 months × 1 weekend (2 days) × 2 drill periods.
- Each of the 48 must be "adjudicated": either **completed** or an **Authorized Absence (AA)**.
- Limit of **24 AAs in any 12-month period**.
- Reschedule and muster in **EDM** on **NSIPS**.
- Don't forget your annual **PRT/PHA**.

### Active duty orders (AT)

- You must complete **12 days of active service** (2 work weeks + the weekend). Otherwise you need a waiver.
- Can be orders of any type. Typically the bean counters want **AT completed first** (and EAT, extra AT, as well).
- Submit requests to HSM-60 admin/PS. They build a **requirement**.
- Go to **NROWS** to build orders.

## 4. Meet 3710 requirements

NATOPS check, instrument check, and **100 hours**, including **12 night** and **12 instrument**. 50% must be in the aircraft. See OPNAVINST 3710, p. 11-4. Applies to the **FY**.

## 5. Qualifying years toward retirement

Your 20 years count by **anniversary year**, which starts in the month you commissioned (for example, May to May). This is a different calendar from the fiscal year.

- **50 points** makes a "good year". You must accumulate 50 points within *your* anniversary year.
- **15 points** for participation (just for breathing), **1 point per drill**, **1 point per day** of active orders.
- Your point summary is on **BOL → ASOSH/ARPR**. The **NEOPS** report in NSIPS is the complete record of your activity and is what retirement will ultimately be based on. NEOPS summarizes at the end of each anniversary year.
- When you transition from active duty, you must update your ASOSH/ARPR.
- A bad year has no consequences except for your own retirement.
- Completing AT is not required for a good year, but it can be important for reaching 50 points.

**Watch the calendar mismatch.** AT is tracked by fiscal year, points by anniversary year. If you do AT early in one FY (say October) and late in the next (say the following September), one anniversary year (say May to May) can end up with *no* AT in it. In that case, pay close attention to your points for that year.

## 6. Additional participation

### Additional drills (EDM)

| Type | What | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| AFTP | Must be on the flight schedule. Does **not** count as IDT. | 22 per year **(verify)**, 2 per day |
| ATP | Additional training periods | 36 per year **(verify)**, 2 per day |
| RMP | Admin work. Can be telework. | 36 per year, 1 per day |

Maximum of **84 additional drills per year**, so 84 + 48 IDT = **132 drills per year** (66 days at 2 drills a day).

### Other orders

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EAT | Extra AT, up to 29 days/year |
| ADT | Active Duty for Training |
| ADSW | Active Duty for Special Work |
| ADOS | Active Duty for Operational Support |
| Mobilization | Not via NROWS |
| Recall | Not via NROWS |

## Pay and benefits

1. **Drills** pay 1/30 of monthly pay per drill. **Orders** pay 1/30 per day.
2. **Drill:** base pay and flight pay. **Orders:** base pay, flight pay, BAH, BAS.
3. Check your LES for flight pay (about $1,000/month **(verify)**, prorated per 30 days).
4. Retirement pay is calculated on points: more points, more money.
5. **VA disability:** you can receive VA payments as a SELRES, but **not** for days you drill. Ask admin for the squadron's VA claims point of contact.
6. **TRICARE Reserve Select** is available. You're eligible for TRICARE on orders of 30 days or more.
7. **Per diem and travel: DTS**
   - **Drill travel:**
     - **IDT-R:** up to $750 for travel. Funding tends to run out, so it's not guaranteed. No per diem.
     - **IDTT:** inactive duty with travel, for drills at locations other than your primary duty location.
     - **NREL (verify):** book lodging for drill (not through DTS).
   - **Orders:** the Navy pays for travel.
   - **General:** an *authorization* in DTS sets aside money **before** you travel. *Vouchers* are submitted within 5 working days **after** travel, and that's how you get paid. NROWS creates the authorizations for orders.

## Career and advancement

- Submit "Don't Pick Me" letters and other correspondence to the board via **BOL**.
- The SELRES O-5 board (#225) is usually in early March.
- Update your records as needed to get AQDs/NOBCs credited **(verify)**.
- O-4 FITREPs are due in **October**.
